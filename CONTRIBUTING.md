# Contributing

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions require a `Signed-off-by` line certifying the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/):

```
git commit -s
```

The sign-off certifies that the contributor authored the contribution or otherwise has the right
to submit it under the licences below, and that the contribution is public and may be
redistributed.

## Licensing of contributions

A contribution is licensed under the terms applying to the part of the repository it modifies:

| Part | Licence |
|---|---|
| Specification text | CC BY 4.0 and Apache-2.0 |
| `schema/` | Apache-2.0 |

## Forking

This specification may be forked and maintained independently, including commercially. A fork
that diverges normatively should be renamed.

## Implementation reports

Reports of implementation experience, including requirements that proved impractical, are
accepted through the repository issue tracker and are the basis for revisions.
